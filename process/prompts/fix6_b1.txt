A few changes:

1. Change the order of the create/edit plan steps to: 1) Goal, 2) Experience, 3) Schedule (days and long-run day).

2. After the last step, show a plan summary page before going to the calendar, with a button to go to the calendar. The summary shows:
- the goal: race distance, race date, number of weeks, start date
- the schedule: run days and long-run day
- the plan at a glance: starting weekly km, peak weekly km, longest run, total km
- their speed now, if they entered any recent race times: the pace per km of their best time
- an estimated finish time for the race as a range, plus the target pace per km for race day. Base it on their recent race times using the Riegel formula (time2 = time1 x (distance2 / distance1)^1.06). The slow end of the range is what they could run today, the fast end assumes training makes them a few percent faster.
- if they gave no race times, say an estimate needs a recent race time, instead of guessing.
The summary should also be reachable later from the plans page.

3. Remove Week and Month from the top bar, the Week/Month switch on the calendar is enough. Keep one link back to the calendar.
